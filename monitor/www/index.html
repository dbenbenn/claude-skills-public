    Disk in use, by what holds it

    Categories are measured hourly with du at idle I/O priority; the total and free space come from the filesystem every 30 s. Lean build output: every .lake under ~/claude except its packages (the workspace build, mission repos). Lean libraries and toolchains: .lake/packages (Mathlib and friends), ~/.elan, ~/.cache/mathlib. Agent job scratch: ~/.claude/jobs (carve dirs). Repos and sources: ~/claude outside .lake. Caches, /tmp, transcripts: the rest of ~/.cache, /tmp, and ~/.claude outside jobs. The grey rest is whatever the filesystem counts beyond these: the Nix store, the 32 GiB swap file, other home directories. The banner warns below 20 GiB free; the OAI queue stops launching below 40.

    Adding a section

    Any job can add its own section: write ~/claude/monitor/sections/<id>.json, e.g. with python3 ~/.claude/skills/monitor/section.py set my-job --title 'My job' --phase 'Items=3/10'. The monitor skill has the details.
